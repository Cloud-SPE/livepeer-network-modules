## 2026-08-24: `215e8a4` / `1241d76`

Built from `tasks/lpm-v2` at commit `215e8a49aa4e` and published
2026-08-24; every binary is stamped `version=v2.0.0-215e8a49aa4e`,
confirmed by running the published payment-daemon and orch-coordinator
and asking each its own version.

Three earlier sets are superseded — `2666f19` and `e92ce32` from
2026-08-22, and `e9445e8` from earlier on 2026-08-24. The tag has now
moved four times, which is the cost of a constant tag and the reason to
pin digests rather than `v2.0.0`.

This set is the first that carries the payment-path work LOC's
real-process matrix validated, none of which was in the `e9445e8`
images:

- funding coverage — a funding intent funds what it says, and a cached
  session no longer under-funds a larger later request;
- restart recovery — a payer that loses its nonce counter recovers;
- reactive rotation, and proactive rollover at the real 600-ticket
  boundary, claimed only when a rollover actually happened;
- a refused request that can be replayed, and an admitted-then-refused
  exchange that says what it cost.

It also carries everything the `e9445e8` set did: the dev keystore
signing for real, the ceiling-division face-value minimum, the job replay
returning its recorded settlement, and the two paid-session wire shapes.

Anyone still pinning the `e9445e8` digests has images that pass none of
the rotation or funding matrix. That is the second time the record has
had to say this, and the reason the digests below are read back from the
registry rather than copied from a build log.

## Three tags were moved, not created

`payment-daemon`, `protocol-daemon` and `service-registry-daemon` already
had a `v2.0.0` from 2026-04-28, published under an older numbering scheme
that ran v1 through v4. Those images still exist; they are simply no
longer reachable by tag. Their digests are in
`v2.0.0-preexisting-digests.txt`, which is what makes this reversible.

Worth knowing when reading the registry: the `1.4.x` line is **newer** in
wall-clock time than the old `v2.x`–`v4.x` tags. Sorting the tag list by
version number reads the history backwards.

## Pinning by digest

Compose renders `${REGISTRY}/livepeer-<name>:${TAG}`, and a digest cannot
be substituted through `TAG` — `repo:@sha256:...` is not a valid
reference. Pinning therefore means editing the `image:` lines to
`repo@sha256:...` rather than setting a variable. The digests above are
the values to use.

Left as-is rather than reworked, because changing every stack's `image:`
line to a digest-shaped variable is a deployment-ergonomics decision, not
a build one.
